# AI Trading Lessons

- 1. Enter trades at appropriate levels, considering current price action, potential corrections, RSI levels, trend direction, recognizing trend direction before entering a trade.
- 2. Confirm trend direction with multiple technical indicators, economic factors, and market sentiment.
- 3. Implement proper risk management strategies.
- 4. Be patient for the right entry point, avoiding entering too late or too early.
- 5. Monitor the market closely for changes that may require adjusting the position or early exit.
- 6. Enter at levels that take into account potential RSI divergences and ensure the RSI is not at extreme levels.
- 7. Recognize and avoid trap patterns, market manipulation, and RSI divergences.
- 8. Review and evaluate the performance of your trading strategy.
- 9. Make decisions based on data and analysis, not emotions.
- 10. Avoid overtrading and focus on quality trades.
- 11. Upon entering a trade, closely monitor the market for significant changes and adjust the position or exit early if necessary.
- 12. Enter trades only when the market conditions and technical analysis align, avoiding ignoring RSI divergences and entering at inappropriate levels.
- 13. Consider overall market sentiment and economic factors impacting the asset's price.
- 14. Develop and adapt a trading strategy.
- 15. Enter trades at levels that ensure the market context aligns with the technical analysis and fundamental factors.
